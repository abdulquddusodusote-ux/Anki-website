Spaced Repetition Flow (Anki Standard)

  • New Cards (Blue): First time you see a card. Choosing Good moves it to the 10m step. Choosing Easy graduates it immediately to 4 days.
  • Learning Cards (Red): Cards currently being learned. When you press Good on the 10m step, it graduates to Review (1 day)!
  • Review Cards (Green): Mature cards due for review. Intervals scale dynamically based on your recall ease.
